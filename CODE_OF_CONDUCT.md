# HAG Brokers Community Code of Conduct

## Our pledge
We are committed to a welcoming and respectful environment for contributors, users and maintainers regardless of background, identity, experience or role.

## Expected behavior
Be constructive, assume good faith, respect others, and report security concerns privately rather than posting customer data or vulnerabilities publicly.

## Unacceptable behavior
Harassment, threats, personal attacks, discriminatory conduct, doxxing, spam, and disclosure of confidential business or customer information.

## Enforcement
Maintainers may remove content, warn contributors, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban serious or repeated violations. Report conduct issues privately to `mr.asmar.web@gmail.com`; include links and details, avoiding sensitive personal information where possible.

We will handle reports confidentially to the extent practicable, without promising absolute confidentiality.
